# Woodcutting — design (grilling session, 2026-11-11)

Starting values for the Woodcutting Job. Every number lives in config and will be tuned by playtesting. Terms are defined in `../../CONTEXT.md`. All item names are translated (English default + French) through the language files; French names below are the starting translations.

Requires Minecraft 26.3+ (Poplar trees, Dappled Forest, Abandoned Camps).

## Scope & XP rules
- Woodcutting = natural blocks of the vanilla axe tag (`mineable/axe`), **minus** crops (pumpkins, melons, cocoa, bamboo → Farming) and bee nests / beehives (→ Ranching), **plus** leaves and the Nether "leaves" (nether wart block, warped wart block, shroomlight), which are hoe blocks in vanilla. **The block decides the Job, not the tool.**
- Crafted-looking blocks generated in structures (planks in villages and mineshafts…) count. Anything placed by a player gives 0.
- A block only counts when the player breaks it themselves (tool, tool effect, hand). TNT, creepers and leaf decay give 0.
- Stripping a natural log does not make it "placed": it keeps its XP.
- **Grown blocks**: blocks generated by a growth (trees, giant mushrooms, huge fungi, chorus, big dripleaf, spreading vines and glow lichen) are tagged when they grow.
  - Grown over time (even from a sapling planted by a player): full XP.
  - Grown by bone meal (by a player or a dispenser): **×0.25 XP**, and ×0.25 for Mastery too.
  - Motherroot's instant regrowth counts as bone meal (×0.25).
- Spreading plants (vines, glow lichen, big dripleaf) follow the same rule; their base XP is tiny so farming them is pointless.
- Multi-block effects (Treefeller, Great Fell, Boomerang, Evoker's Hatchet, Worldfeller…): full XP and a Cache roll for every block.
- Account Rate: **0.8**.

### Felled Tree
A tree is felled when the player breaks the last natural log of its trunk (the logs of the same wood type connected to the one they broke). Uses the same tree scan as Treefeller. Cutting only the top does not fell the tree. Everything that says "per tree" (Rhythm, Birch / Cherry / Verdant axes, Replant, Motherroot, tree Quests) counts Felled Trees.

## Level curve & milestones
Shared with every Job (see `mining.md`): XP to next level = `50 × L^1.3`; milestone Rune Cache every 5 levels.

## Rune Caches from woodcutting
- Drop chance per block = `XP × 0.05%` (≈ 1% per simple oak tree, 1% per creaking heart).
- Contents: 60% Woodcutting Runes, 40% general passive Runes.

## Axe base stats
Default by Harvest Tier, overridable per axe. Damage follows vanilla axes.

| | Speed | Durability | Damage / attack speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| stone-tier | 4 | 131 | 9 / 0.8 |
| iron-tier | 6 | 250 | 9 / 0.9 |
| diamond-tier (Rare) | 8 | 1,561 | 9 / 1.0 |
| Epic / Legendary | 12 | 2,031 | 10 / 1.0 |

Kills with an axe give Combat XP (weapon decides). Axe vs sword balance is revisited when Combat is designed.

## Mastery
+1 Capacity every **1,000 logs** (logs, wood, Nether stems, giant mushroom stems, chorus plants; leaves excluded), up to +75% of base Capacity. Bone-meal-grown logs count ×0.25. To be retuned in playtests.

## Axes
Players start with the vanilla wooden axe (no Capacity). First Rune axes unlock at Woodcutting Level 2. No Starter Recipe.

### Relics
Only for players at Woodcutting Level 25+. Each also drops, ultra-rarely, from Legendary-tier Woodcutting Quests. Abandoned Camps are common, so their Relic odds per chest are tiny.

| Relic (EN / FR) | Trait | Structure source |
|---|---|---|
| Timberfall / Chute-des-Géants | Fells a whole tree with no block cap (same wood type); drops go straight to the inventory | Woodland Mansion chests, Abandoned Camp hidden chest |
| Evoker's Hatchet / Hachette de l'Évocateur | Click in air: a line of evoker fangs fells trees over 12 blocks and hurts mobs | Woodland Mansion chests |
| Creaking Axe / Hache du Grinçant | While held, hostile mobs looking at you freeze | Abandoned Camps (Pale Garden variant) |
| Motherroot / Racine-Mère | Every Felled Tree regrows instantly in place (×0.25 XP) | Buried treasure chests |

### Fused Relics
No Blueprint needed. Each recipe: the 2 Relics + 2 Legendary Cores + 4 netherite blocks + 2 beacons + 1 heavy core + 1 enchanted golden apple.

| Fused Relic (EN / FR) | Made from | Trait |
|---|---|---|
| Worldfeller / Fléau des forêts | Timberfall + Evoker's Hatchet | Click in air: a wave of fangs fells every whole tree in a 16-block cone; drops go to the inventory |
| Grovewarden / Gardien du Bosquet | Creaking Axe + Motherroot | Regeneration II + Resistance I in forest biomes; hostile mobs looking at you freeze; Felled Trees regrow |

**Treefeller rules** (also used by the Felled Tree scan, Tempest Axe, Great Fell, Boomerang): follows natural logs of **the same wood type** as the broken block, connected, **at or above** the broken block only, plus the canopy blocks touching them (leaves, wart blocks, shroomlight). Everything counts towards the block cap, so packed forests (Nether, jungle, dark oak) are never cut whole.

## Woodcutting armor ("Rhythm")
**Rhythm** (FR: Cadence): each Felled Tree adds one charge, max 10; all charges reset after 30 s without felling a tree. Each piece's Trait scales from 0% (no charge) to 100% (10 charges). Protection follows vanilla by Rarity, overridable. Capacity fixed (no Mastery).

Unlock levels: Common 3, Uncommon 7, Rare 12, Epic 18, Legendary by Blueprint.

## Woodcutting Quests
Quest Types at launch:

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Collect | "Cut 64 spruce logs" |
| Place | "Cut 40 logs in a Dark Forest" |
| Variety | "Cut 5 different wood types" |
| Tool | "Fell 20 trees with a Jungle Axe" |
| Reforestation | "Grow 10 trees from saplings" (only successful growths count) |

Quest Tier (Common → Legendary) multiplies the objective (×1 / ×1.5 / ×2 / ×3 / ×5) and improves the rewards; see `design-decisions.md`. Legendary-tier Woodcutting Quests can, ultra-rarely, reward axe Relics; high Tiers can reward the Treetop Stride Blueprint.

Later: Challenge ("fell a giant dark oak in under 10 s"), Rhythm ("reach 10 Rhythm charges", high Tiers only).
